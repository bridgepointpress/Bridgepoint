# The Baseline — Weekly Newsletter for Bridge and Baseline

One email a week, published the same day as the video. Built to cost fifteen minutes because every input already exists in the pre-script checklist — no new writing on a Friday.

---

## 1. Platform and setup

**Platform:** Kit (formerly ConvertKit), free tier — free to 10,000 subscribers.

Setup is four items:

1. One sign-up form, promise line: *"One email a week. The fix, the ladder, one thing to try."*
2. Double opt-in switched on (UK audience, GDPR, keeps the list clean).
3. One welcome email (below) — no other automation. Don't build sequences for a list of zero.
4. Sender name: **Bridge and Baseline**. Not a personal name. The brand is the trust object at this stage.

### Welcome email (sent once, on sign-up)

> You're on the list. Here's what to expect: one email a week, usually the day the video goes up. It covers the week's friction point, the Fix Ladder from £0 upward, and one thing you can try before next week without buying anything. Nothing else. If a week's email isn't useful, the next one probably will be, because the friction changes every week. Play more. Work less.

---

## 2. Issue structure — four blocks, always the same

| Block | Content | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. This week's fix | One paragraph: the friction, what the fix was, what it unlocked. Link to the video. | Weekly |
| 2. The Fix Ladder | Four lines, £0 → £100+, product names. Phase 2 affiliate links live here with a disclosure line underneath. | Weekly |
| 3. Try this before next week | One free, do-today action tied to the week's friction — the £0 tier or a fragment of the before-measurement. Keeps the email worth opening even if they already watched the video. | Weekly |
| 4. From the bench | Two or three honest, unpolished sentences — what went wrong filming, what's being tested, how the hands are. In Playing Through It weeks, this becomes a short series note instead. | First week of each month only |

**Sign-off, every issue:** *Play more. Work less.*

---

## 3. The generation system

Every block maps directly from the pre-script checklist already completed before filming:

| Newsletter block | Comes from |
|---|---|
| This week's fix | The friction point + the "what it unlocks" line |
| Fix Ladder | The four-tier ladder, verbatim from the script |
| Try this before next week | The £0 tier, or the before-measurement reframed as a self-test |
| Subject line | The friction, stated plainly, under 45 characters |

**Workflow:** video script finalised → copy the four fields into the template → adjust two sentences so it reads as an email, not a script → schedule.

**Subject line rule:** name the friction, never the fix.
- ✅ "Why barre chords hurt" — gets opened.
- ❌ "The nut slot fix you need" — reads as a pitch.

---

## 4. Growth

- Sign-up link in every video description from Video 1, one line, below the Fix Ladder.
- Same line on the channel About page.
- The Arthritis Checklist PDF becomes the lead magnet once ready — "free download, sent by email" converts better than "join my newsletter." Until then, the plain promise line is enough.
- No pop-ups, no "last chance" language, no growth tactics that would embarrass the channel. The list grows at the speed of trust, same as everything else.

---

## 5. The one rule that protects this system

**No new email infrastructure until the list passes 100 people.**

No segments, no tags, no Playing Through It sub-list, no six-email welcome sequence — not until there's a list big enough for any of it to matter. Until 100 subscribers, the only job is publishing the weekly issue on time.
