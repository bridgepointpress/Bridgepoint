# Bridge and Baseline — YouTube Content Research
**Date:** June 2026

---

## Channel Context (from existing docs)

- **Niche:** Guitar setup, ergonomics, and adaptive playing for adult guitarists aged 35–65
- **Audience:** Players fighting friction — arthritis, reduced grip, returning players, post-injury hands
- **Core format:** One friction point → PM insight → Fix Ladder ($0 / $6–20 / $25–100 / $100+) → before/after demo → 30 sec uninterrupted playing
- **Mission:** "Luxury is the absence of friction. Easier is not a shortcut — easier is the point."
- **Already scripted (Videos 1–9):** Setup basics, arthritis (PTI Ep 1), pawn shop rescue, barre chords, $4k mistake, light strings, ergonomics, identity video, PTI series intro

## Trending Topics & Keywords

**High search volume, well-matched to channel format:**
- "guitar action too high" — extremely high search, clear DIY angle
- "guitar buzzing / fret buzz fix" — one of the highest-volume guitar queries
- "guitar setup cost" — emotional search; audience wants to know if they're overpaying
- "how to play guitar with arthritis" — growing search, underserved in quality content
- "guitar for beginners over 50" — rising demographic
- "silent guitar practice apartment" — urban lifestyle trend, steady growth
- "short scale guitar benefits" — growing interest, especially among older players
- "when to change guitar strings" — perennial beginner entry point
- "what is neck relief" — confusing term, high frustration signal
- "how to intonate a guitar" — high search, almost no beginner-accessible content

**Emerging / underserved angles (2025–2026):**
- Carpal tunnel + guitar (distinct from arthritis — compression vs. inflammation; no one covers this clearly)
- Guitar humidity and seasonal setup drift (recurring need, no strong beginner resource exists)
- Ergonomic guitars and body weight (Strandberg-type coverage mostly ignores the 45-year-old casual player)
- Spatial audio / headphone amp comparison (Boss Waza Air, Fender Mustang Micro trending in silent practice)

## Content Gaps and Underexplored Angles

1. **Carpal tunnel as a distinct condition** — forums are full of this question; no channel addresses it clearly and directly. High-priority gap.
2. **Seasonal/humidity content** — guitar setups drift in winter. Repeatable, searchable, and builds returning viewer habit.
3. **The luthier vs. DIY decision** — high emotional charge for this audience (cost anxiety + trust issues); the channel is perfectly positioned to answer it neutrally.
4. **"Viewer rescue" format** — invite a subscriber to send their guitar, document the full fix on camera. Builds community, generates social proof, creates shareable content.
5. **Playing during a flare-up** — most adaptive playing content is setup-focused; no one covers what to do on a bad hand day.
6. **Guitar size and body fit** — short-scale, parlour, and 3/4 guitars are covered in gear content but almost never framed as a comfort/ergonomics fix. Strong channel fit.
7. **One-handed and partial-limb playing** — Django is already in the channel DNA. An episode here would be the most shareable PTI video and draw coverage beyond the guitar niche.

### Content Gaps and Underexplored Angles (new)

1. **Presbyopia / aging eyesight** — a friction point that affects nearly the entire target demographic by their late 40s, currently absent from the channel entirely. Highest-priority gap found this pass.
2. **Left-handed adult players** — a whole audience segment with zero channel coverage; strong fit with the "no default body" philosophy.
3. **Hearing health as a reason for quiet practice** — reframes an existing content cluster rather than requiring new production infrastructure; cheap to add.
4. **The tuning-peg-on-short-scale problem** — a self-inflicted gap: the channel recommends short-scale guitars as a fix elsewhere in the backlog without acknowledging this specific downside, which weakens trust with a well-informed viewer.
