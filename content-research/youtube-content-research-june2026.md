## 1. Timely Hook — Act Before It Goes Cold

### Keith Richards Arthritis Coverage (March 2026)
Guitar World and Radio Plus ran pieces in March 2026 on how Richards (now 82) has adapted his playing around arthritis — fewer notes, different voicings, compensating through musicality rather than technique.

**Why it matters:** This is a rare celebrity peg that fits the channel perfectly. It's fading but still indexable.

**Suggested video:**
- **"How Keith Richards Plays Guitar With Arthritis (And What You Can Steal From It)"**
- Hook: "He's not playing less. He's playing smarter."
- Thumbnail: Richards photo + channel's PTI series card
- Angle: Use the celebrity story as an opening, then pivot to the Fix Ladder. What Richards does intuitively (lighter touch, open tunings, capo use), the channel can systematise.
- Estimated appeal: **High** — celebrity name drives discovery from outside the core audience; PTI framing retains them

## 3. New Format Opportunity — "Rules for Older Beginners"

A YouTube video titled **"The ONLY 10 Rules for Older Beginners"** is surfacing prominently in searches for "guitar beginners over 50" and "returning guitarist." This list/rules format is consistently high-CTR for the 35–65 demographic.

**Gap in current plan:** Every planned video is a deep-dive. No punchy list-format video exists.

**Suggested addition:**
- **"10 Things I'd Tell My 50-Year-Old Self Before Picking Up Guitar Again"**
- Hook: "Most beginner advice was written for teenagers. This isn't that."
- Thumbnail: Split — teenage beginner illustration vs. adult hands on guitar
- Format: Faster pace, one tip per 60–90 seconds, designed to also cut into 10 Shorts
- Estimated appeal: **High** — high discovery potential, easy to produce, natural Shorts factory

## 5. New Sub-Angle — "Which Guitar Works Best for Silent Practice?"

A buyer's guide titled **"10 Best Guitar Brands for Silent Practice Setups in 2026"** appeared in search results. The existing plan covers *gear* (headphone amps, foam muting) but not the *instrument* selection angle.

**Gap:** No planned video answers "should I buy a different guitar for apartment/silent playing?"

**Suggested addition:**
- **"The Best Guitar for Silent Practice (It's Not What You Think)"**
- Hook: "The gear matters. But the guitar matters more."
- Angle: Solid-body electrics produce less acoustic bleed; semi-hollows are worse than you'd expect; Yamaha SLG as the premium option; parlour acoustics vs. dreadnoughts for quiet acoustic playing
- Fix Ladder: £0 (existing electric) → £15 foam mute → £80 solid-body budget electric → £400 Yamaha SLG
- Estimated appeal: **Medium-High** — connects the ergonomics/body-size thread with the silent practice thread

## 6. "Realistic Expectations" Framing — Trending Search Pattern

GearNews ran a piece in 2026 titled **"Guitar Practice in 2026: How to Realistically Improve."** Search data from the returning-guitarist query shows consistent pairing of "improve" with "realistic" and "busy life."

**Gap in current plan:** Practice Systems videos are framed around *techniques* (sprints, logging). None is explicitly framed around *expectation-setting*.

**Suggested addition:**
- **"How Much Progress Can You Actually Expect? (Honest Answer for Adult Learners)"**
- Hook: "Every resource promises you'll be playing songs in 30 days. They're lying."
- Angle: PM-style milestone thinking applied to realistic timelines; benchmark by age/hand condition; counter-programming against over-promising tutorial culture
- Estimated appeal: **High** — this audience has been burned by false promises; honesty converts strongly

## 7. Short-Scale Framing Evolution

Multiple 2026 buyer guides now frame short-scale guitars explicitly **"for adults"** (not just "for small hands" or "for kids"). Sweetwater's "Best Electric Guitars of 2026" features short-scale models prominently, and Tone Authority published **"Top 10 Short-Scale Electric Guitars for Adults (2026 Buyer's Guide)."**

The existing plan has the video idea ("The Guitar I Wish I'd Bought at 50") and the keyword ("short scale guitar benefits"). **What's changed:** the market is now actively using "for adults" as a search modifier, which means the title should reflect this shift.

**Title refinement:**
- Existing: "The Guitar I Wish I'd Bought at 50"
- Stronger: **"Short Scale Guitars for Adults: What Nobody Tells You"**
- Or: **"Why Short Scale Guitars Aren't Just for Kids (A 50-Year-Old's Honest Review)"**
