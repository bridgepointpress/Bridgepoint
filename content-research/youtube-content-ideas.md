## Channel Context (from existing docs)

- **Niche:** Guitar setup, ergonomics, and adaptive playing for adult guitarists aged 35–65
- **Audience:** Players fighting friction — arthritis, reduced grip, returning players, post-injury hands
- **Core format:** One friction point → PM insight → Fix Ladder ($0 / $6–20 / $25–100 / $100+) → before/after demo → 30 sec uninterrupted playing
- **Mission:** "Luxury is the absence of friction. Easier is not a shortcut — easier is the point."
- **Already scripted (Videos 1–9):** Setup basics, arthritis (PTI Ep 1), pawn shop rescue, barre chords, $4k mistake, light strings, ergonomics, identity video, PTI series intro

## Content Gaps and Underexplored Angles

1. **Carpal tunnel as a distinct condition** — forums are full of this question; no channel addresses it clearly and directly. High-priority gap.
2. **Seasonal/humidity content** — guitar setups drift in winter. Repeatable, searchable, and builds returning viewer habit.
3. **The luthier vs. DIY decision** — high emotional charge for this audience (cost anxiety + trust issues); the channel is perfectly positioned to answer it neutrally.
4. **"Viewer rescue" format** — invite a subscriber to send their guitar, document the full fix on camera. Builds community, generates social proof, creates shareable content.
5. **Playing during a flare-up** — most adaptive playing content is setup-focused; no one covers what to do on a bad hand day.
6. **Guitar size and body fit** — short-scale, parlour, and 3/4 guitars are covered in gear content but almost never framed as a comfort/ergonomics fix. Strong channel fit.
7. **One-handed and partial-limb playing** — Django is already in the channel DNA. An episode here would be the most shareable PTI video and draw coverage beyond the guitar niche.

## Content Research Update — 19 July 2026

Scheduled research pass. No spreadsheet backlog file was found in this repo to cross-check against, so treat the ideas below as candidates to screen against the v10 backlog before scheduling — some may already be planned.

### Trending Topics & Keywords (new signal since June)

- **"Guitarist as athlete" framing** — coverage of ageing professional players (Keith Richards, Alex Lifeson, Joe Satriani) managing arthritis is getting fresh press; the framing of warm-up and pacing as preventive maintenance rather than a response to injury is gaining traction and maps directly onto the PM lens.
- **Neck profile for joint comfort** — "slim C profile" and scale-length comparisons are showing up in arthritis-and-guitar coverage but aren't yet framed as a standalone, measurable Fix Ladder decision.
- **"Am I too old to learn guitar"** — a persistent, high-emotion search phrase with a clear yes/no answer; currently answered by generic lesson sites, not by anyone using a Fix Ladder to make the answer concrete.
- **Sharp pain vs. workable discomfort** — health-adjacent coverage is drawing a hard line between "stop now" pain and normal effort discomfort; distinct from the planned "Playing Through a Flare-Up" episode (that one is about adapting on a bad day, this is about recognising the line before a flare-up starts).
- **Adaptive chord-assist devices** (ChordBuddy-type products, one-handed adaptations) — steady interest but almost no neutral, multi-tier review exists; most coverage is either promotional or inspirational human-interest, not practical.
- **Small-hands / short-scale market validation** — 2026 buying guides now treat short-scale as a serious adult category rather than a kids'-guitar compromise, confirming and strengthening the angle already in the backlog (Ep: "The Guitar I Wish I'd Bought at 50").
- **Shorts algorithm shift** — watch-time-per-impression has replaced swipe rate as the ranking signal; 30–45 second Shorts with original voiceover now outperform sub-15-second clips and trending-audio cuts. Relevant to format, not topic: existing before/after measurement clips should run closer to 30–45 seconds with the creator's own voice rather than text-only.

### Gaps Confirmed or Newly Surfaced

- **Preventive framing is missing** — nearly all existing arthritis/PTI content is reactive (fix the pain that's already there); nothing yet frames warm-up and pacing as scheduled maintenance. Strong fit for the PM lens and currently has no direct channel precedent.
- **No "line in the sand" video** — the channel has flare-up content but nothing that helps a player recognise the line before it's crossed. Low production complexity, high relevance.
- **Top-of-funnel search intent is underused** — "Am I too old" and similar broad-audience queries aren't currently targeted; these are lower-effort entry points that could feed the more specialised series.
- **Neutral adaptive-device coverage is genuinely rare** — most existing coverage online is either promotional or purely emotional; a fair, tiered test would be distinctive and hard to replicate.
