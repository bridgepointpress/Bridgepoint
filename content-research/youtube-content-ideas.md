## Channel Context (from existing docs)

- **Niche:** Guitar setup, ergonomics, and adaptive playing for adult guitarists aged 35–65
- **Audience:** Players fighting friction — arthritis, reduced grip, returning players, post-injury hands
- **Core format:** One friction point → PM insight → Fix Ladder ($0 / $6–20 / $25–100 / $100+) → before/after demo → 30 sec uninterrupted playing
- **Mission:** "Luxury is the absence of friction. Easier is not a shortcut — easier is the point."

- Spatial audio / headphone amp comparison (Boss Waza Air, Fender Mustang Micro trending in silent practice)

## Content Gaps and Underexplored Angles

1. **Carpal tunnel as a distinct condition** — forums are full of this question; no channel addresses it clearly and directly. High-priority gap.
2. **Seasonal/humidity content** — guitar setups drift in winter. Repeatable, searchable, and builds returning viewer habit.
3. **The luthier vs. DIY decision** — high emotional charge for this audience (cost anxiety + trust issues); the channel is perfectly positioned to answer it neutrally.
4. **"Viewer rescue" format** — invite a subscriber to send their guitar, document the full fix on camera. Builds community, generates social proof, creates shareable content.
5. **Playing during a flare-up** — most adaptive playing content is setup-focused; no one covers what to do on a bad hand day.
6. **Guitar size and body fit** — short-scale, parlour, and 3/4 guitars are covered in gear content but almost never framed as a comfort/ergonomics fix. Strong channel fit.
7. **One-handed and partial-limb playing** — Django is already in the channel DNA. An episode here would be the most shareable PTI video and draw coverage beyond the guitar niche.

# Research Update — September 20, 2026

*Web-researched trend pass. Cross-checked against the ideas already logged above to avoid duplication — most new signals here extend existing series (Silent & Stealth Practice, Playing Through It) rather than replace them.*

## New Trending Signals Since June

- **"Guitars for small hands" is a saturated search term with zero ergonomics framing.** Results are wall-to-wall gear listicles (Guitar World, Guitar Lobby, MusicMentor) ranking products by spec sheet. None of them explain nut width, scale length, and neck profile as three independent, measurable variables — the channel's diagnostic-first format has no real competitor here. **Highest-priority new opportunity this round.**
- **Headphone amp market has refreshed.** Fender Mustang Micro Plus and Boss Katana 50 Gen 3 are now the models showing up in "best of 2026" roundups, ahead of the units referenced in the existing Silent Practice ideas above. The channel's own comparison video will read as dated within a year of publishing — this is a content-freshness problem worth planning for, not just a one-off video.
- **2026 market commentary (Chesbro Music, Accio) flags "instruments that fit modern life"** — quiet practice, compact rigs, easy recording — as a stated purchase driver for adult hobbyists specifically. This validates the Silent & Stealth Practice series as a growth lane rather than a niche detour, and suggests a *space*-constrained companion angle (small flats, shared rooms) distinct from the existing *noise*-constrained angle.
- **Adult hobbyists are buying fewer premium instruments and more affordable units** (per current market-trend coverage) — reinforces that Budget Guitar Rescue and full Fix Ladder framing (not gear-flex content) is the right growth bet, not just a values choice.
- **Neuropathy + guitar remains an open gap.** A direct search for guitar-neuropathy content returned nothing — this confirms the gap already flagged in the June research and in the PTI Ep 6 idea above. Still no direct competitor.
