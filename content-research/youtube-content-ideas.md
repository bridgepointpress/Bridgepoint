## Channel Context (from existing docs)

- **Niche:** Guitar setup, ergonomics, and adaptive playing for adult guitarists aged 35–65
- **Audience:** Players fighting friction — arthritis, reduced grip, returning players, post-injury hands
- **Core format:** One friction point → PM insight → Fix Ladder ($0 / $6–20 / $25–100 / $100+) → before/after demo → 30 sec uninterrupted playing
- **Mission:** "Luxury is the absence of friction. Easier is not a shortcut — easier is the point."

## Content Gaps and Underexplored Angles

1. **Carpal tunnel as a distinct condition** — forums are full of this question; no channel addresses it clearly and directly. High-priority gap.
2. **Seasonal/humidity content** — guitar setups drift in winter. Repeatable, searchable, and builds returning viewer habit.
3. **The luthier vs. DIY decision** — high emotional charge for this audience (cost anxiety + trust issues); the channel is perfectly positioned to answer it neutrally.
4. **"Viewer rescue" format** — invite a subscriber to send their guitar, document the full fix on camera. Builds community, generates social proof, creates shareable content.
5. **Playing during a flare-up** — most adaptive playing content is setup-focused; no one covers what to do on a bad hand day.
6. **Guitar size and body fit** — short-scale, parlour, and 3/4 guitars are covered in gear content but almost never framed as a comfort/ergonomics fix. Strong channel fit.
7. **One-handed and partial-limb playing** — Django is already in the channel DNA. An episode here would be the most shareable PTI video and draw coverage beyond the guitar niche.

# Content Research Update — 17 July 2026

*Everything above this line is preserved from the June 2026 research pass. This section adds new trending topics, ideas, and gaps identified since then — it does not replace or duplicate the above.*

## Trending Topics & Keywords (New Since June)

- **"Silicone finger caps for guitar arthritis"** — steady product-search demand; 2026 product cycle has shifted toward thinner, more ventilated designs that claim to preserve string feel rather than just pad the fingertip. No neutral, non-affiliate-driven comparison exists yet.
- **Short-scale destigmatization** — 2026 coverage (short-scale basses especially) is actively pushing back on the "beginner/training wheels" framing, citing established players who use short-scale as a permanent choice. This is a narrative shift the channel can ride rather than originate.
- **Adult absolute beginners (40+, never played before)** — search and press coverage confirm this is a distinct, underserved segment from "returning player after a gap." Most existing adult-guitar content still targets returning players or assumes teenage learning patterns.
- **Fingerstyle/hybrid picking as joint-pain mitigation** — recurring claim across arthritis-and-guitar content that plucking is gentler than flatpicking on sore joints, but no one has actually measured or demonstrated this on camera.
- **Guitar setup fundamentals (fret buzz, neck relief, intonation, action)** — still the highest-volume, most stable search cluster in the whole niche; confirms the channel's existing Setup Basics series is aimed correctly and these topics are evergreen, not a passing trend.
- **YouTube Shorts algorithm shift (2026)** — watch-time-per-impression has replaced swipe-past rate as the dominant Shorts ranking signal; sub-15s clips are losing reach even at high retention, 30–45s is the current sweet spot, original audio/voiceover now carries a ranking bonus over trending sound, and 2–3 uploads/week are outperforming daily posting on a per-video basis. This affects how the channel should cut Reels/Shorts from existing long-form footage, not what to film.

## New Video Ideas (Output A)

### 1. Starting Guitar at 45? What Nobody Tells First-Time Adult Beginners
- **The friction:** Adult beginners with no playing history don't know what "normal" early progress looks like, and most beginner content is paced and framed for teenagers.
- **The fix angle:** Expectation-setting and starting-point assessment, not a single physical fix — establishes a baseline before technique even enters the picture.
- **Fix Ladder:**
  - Free — a five-minute baseline check (hand stretch, reach, grip) before ever touching a fretboard
  - Affordable £5–20 — clip-on tuner, so tuning isn't the first frustration
  - Quality upgrade £25–100 — a proper setup at time of purchase rather than assuming factory setup is fine
  - Premium £100+ — an instrument matched to hand size and reach, not simply "the cheapest one in the shop"

### 2. Finger Caps for Guitar: Do They Actually Help With Arthritis?
- **The friction:** Fingertip pain from string pressure, especially during the callus-building phase or a flare-up, and no clear, non-salesy answer on whether finger caps genuinely help.
- **The fix angle:** Product demonstration with honest before/after — playability and comfort, not just "does it work."
- **Fix Ladder:**
  - Free — shorter practice intervals with deliberate rest, before reaching for any product
  - Affordable £5–15 — silicone/gel finger caps
  - Quality upgrade £25–100 — lighter-gauge strings and lower action paired with caps, addressing the cause alongside the symptom
  - Premium — not required for this friction; note plainly that this is a Free/Affordable-tier fix, not a spending problem
- **Adaptive relevance:** Directly serves the arthritis and flare-up audience; strong PTI-series fit.

### 3. Fingerstyle or Pick? Which Is Actually Gentler on Sore Hands
- **The friction:** Conflicting advice on whether switching from a pick to fingers reduces joint strain, with no one actually demonstrating the difference.
- **The fix angle:** Side-by-side technique comparison, measured rather than asserted.
- **Fix Ladder:**
  - Free — technique switch alone, no purchase required
  - Affordable £5–20 — thumb pick
  - Quality upgrade £25–100 — fingerpicks sized and fitted properly
  - Premium £100+ — custom-fit adaptive pick or orthotic-style aid for players who need one
- **Adaptive relevance:** Speaks directly to arthritis, trigger finger, and reduced grip strength; complements the existing PTI series without duplicating an episode already on the board.

### 4. Short Scale Was Never the "Beginner" Guitar
- **The friction:** Hesitation or embarrassment about choosing a short-scale instrument because of a lingering "starter guitar" stigma.
- **The fix angle:** Reframing plus measurable ergonomics — shorter scale length reduces fret spacing and string tension, which is a comfort upgrade, not a downgrade.
- **Fix Ladder:** Pull directly from the Short Scale Guitars 2026 doc's price-tier breakdown (in-house brands at the low end through to purpose-built short-scale instruments at the premium tier).
- **Adaptive relevance:** Reduced reach and lower tension benefit arthritis, reduced grip strength, and smaller hands alike.

### 5. The Bad-Advice List: Guitar Advice That Makes Pain Worse
- **The friction:** Well-meaning but harmful advice circulating in forums and comment sections — "play through it," "you'll build calluses eventually," "any guitar is fine to learn on" — that keeps players stuck or in pain.
- **The fix angle:** Myth-busting format; each myth gets the PM reframe (this is a setup problem or a pacing problem, not a toughness problem).
- **Fix Ladder:** Naturally pulls examples from across all four tiers as each myth is corrected, making it a strong index/hub video that can link out to deeper episodes.
- **Adaptive relevance:** Directly validates the arthritis, flare-up, and returning-player audience by naming and dismissing advice that isn't serving them.

### 6. Neck Profile and Nut Width: The Ergonomics Nobody Measures
- **The friction:** Players fixate on scale length and body size but never learn that neck shape and nut width materially change how much the hand has to stretch and curl.
- **The fix angle:** Explainer plus hands-on comparison across a few common profiles (C-shape, D-shape, wide vs. narrow nut).
- **Fix Ladder:**
  - Free — how to measure your own guitar's nut width and neck depth at home
  - Affordable £5–20 — N/A as a standalone purchase; pairs with setup-tier fixes
  - Quality upgrade £25–100 — a setup adjustment (nut work) to fine-tune feel within the current instrument
  - Premium £100+ — choosing a next instrument by neck spec rather than brand or looks
- **Adaptive relevance:** Directly useful for smaller hands, reduced finger spread, and joint stiffness; fills a real gap since the channel has covered body size and scale length but not neck/nut geometry.

## Content Gaps and Underexplored Angles (New)

1. **First-time adult beginners vs. returning players** — the channel currently speaks mostly to returning players and existing owners of "unfinished" guitars; the never-played-before 40+ segment is confirmed as a distinct, growing, and underserved audience.
2. **Finger caps / gel protectors as a named product category** — arthritis content repeatedly mentions this fix, but the channel has no episode that actually tests and reports on it.
3. **Picking-hand technique for joint pain** — nearly all existing adaptive content (including this channel's own backlog) is fretting-hand focused; picking-hand comfort is comparatively untouched.
4. **Neck profile and nut width** — scale length and body size are covered; neck geometry as its own ergonomics lever is not.
5. **A myth-busting/hub format** — the channel has many individual fix videos but no single video that collects and corrects bad advice, which could also serve as a strong evergreen entry point for new viewers.
